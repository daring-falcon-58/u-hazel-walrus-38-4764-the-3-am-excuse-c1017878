# Candidates — The 3 AM Excuse

37 candidate lines, each scored 1–5 against the published judge brief, in the
brief's own order (earlier criteria dominate ties):

1. **On-subject** — an excuse a hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign a
   transaction at 3 AM.
2. **Originality** — a fresh angle; NOT the usual jokes about batteries,
   firmware updates or cold storage.
3. **Wallet's voice** — the device speaking, with its own priorities and
   grudges, not a generic assistant or a person.
4. **The hour** — 3 AM itself is the joke; the line could not be pasted onto
   a 3 PM transaction.
5. **Economy** — one tight line under 200 characters, every word earning its
   place, no explaining its own joke.

Char counts verified by script (`python3 -c` over this file's list); all are
under 200 characters. Scores are the author's blind read against the brief,
recorded before picking the winner.

## The winner

> **"I have one job: keep your coins safe from strangers. At 3 AM, statistically, the stranger is you."** — 97 chars — **25/25**

Why it wins: the inversion is the joke — the wallet's whole job is keeping
coins safe from strangers, and at 3 AM the stranger is the owner. It is
on-subject (refusal to sign, 3 AM named), original (no battery / firmware /
cold-storage cliché; the threat is the owner, not a hacker), in the device's
own deadpan voice ("statistically"), the hour is load-bearing (at 3 PM the
owner is not the stranger), and it is tight at 97 characters.
